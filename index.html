    How the system works

    1. Board: tap a room: red (occupied), amber (guest left, check pending), white (cleaning), green (ready).
    2. Check: tap an amber room and tick any damage, missing items or complaints. Issues stay on the room and show in Details until you mark them fixed.
    3. Available: shows every green room and its rate. Edit any rate and it saves at once.
    4. Details: occupancy, value of rooms, floor totals and the activity log.
    5. Everything is saved in this browser on this device. Use the same device each shift.
